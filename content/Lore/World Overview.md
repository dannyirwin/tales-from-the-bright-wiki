The people of [[IDK YET WORLD]] build their city-state nations scattered across the continent. The [[Wilds]] outside settlements are a constant deadly pressure, making long term nations and large scale trade difficult to achieve. Instead these communities all find their own ways of surviving, often with blessings from their own holy traditions, occasionally with great relics and artifacts from before the [[Divine Calamity - The War of the Gods|Divine Calamity]], or with magic-imbued [[Divine Illumination|Blackstone]], a commonplace source of magic and blessings used all around the world.

Belief, tho not necisarly worship, of the gods is commonplace here. This is owing to the once-a-decade [[Stonewake Festival]], the night when the gods briefly descend to the [[Mundane Plane|Mundane World]] to once again flood it with their blessings.  This is the most important day for all of society all around the world, but especially to [[Lumenhold]] (Seat of the [[Arecene]] region). Built on the very spot of the gods decennial descent, the city sprawls over the peak of [[Mount Steradian]] and is a beacon of prosperity and triumph against the wilds. This mountain is also the source of the [[Divine Illumination|Blackstone]] which absorbs and stores [[Divine Illumination|Illumination]] during the visitation. The flow of both Blackstone and pilgrims follow a decade long cycle: the start of the decade is always marked with prosperity and a time to take ground from the wilds, and where austerity and hardship often ring in the end of the decade as stores of divinity dwindle. 

This fragile balance between the gods and the wilds make traversing the wilds both very difficult and very important. Pilgrims travel the roads to bring divinity to their homes, to make good money trading or as protection, even just to lay eyes on a true god for one brief evening.

Our story begins before [[Stonewake Festival|Stonewake]] in the year 2489.  Our adventures, like countless other merchants, worshipers, adventures, and pilgrims have joined one  caravan of many to ascend [[Mount Steradian]] to [[Lumenhold]] to attend the festival. It has been a hard end to the decade but hope is high to ring in the new year

## The Nations of the World

The city-state nations very significantly place to place (read: Room for collaborative world building for your character). They all need to figure out defense from the wilds, and how to provide resources to their denizens, but beyond that basically anything goes within the confines. Some cities boast heavy magic use while others rely on stolid infrastructure. A city could have a militia or even ancient artifact at their defense, while another could exclusively use their natural surroundings too keep safe. Some might be very diverse, some with a more unified heritage. 

## Magic in this world 

Simple holy magic is very commonplace, most cantrips and low level spells are somewhat achievable, but more powerful or non-holy magics are more rare. You certainly can have a character that uses other forms of magic (any class is valid) but they will be somewhat unusual in settlements that largely rely on the divine. Otherwise settlements will all have their own relationships and beliefs about magic. In the wilds magic is common and unpredictable

## Common historical knowledge

The [[Divine Calamity - The War of the Gods]] ended 2489 years ago. The [[Gods]] fought the [[The Void]] over the fate of the world. It is known that the old gods treated the [[Mundane Plane|material world]] like play things, life of the people of [[Mundane Plane|the Mundane]] was in constant upheaval amongst the divine machinations. The Gods, lead by [[Arecenia - The Bright Mother]], were able to defeat the Void.  In penance and to protect the world, [[Arecenia - The Bright Mother|Bright Mother]] forbade all gods from interfering in the world, save for their once-a-decade procession when they pour out their divinity and leave again to rejuvenate their divinity.

The [[Heroic Age]] casts a long shadow over the [[Bright Era - 0 to 2489 (Present)]]. Not too much is really know (it has been taboo for millennia) and yet it is still viewed as both a dark age of war and destruction but also the golden age of epics and myths. Some modern heroes and noble houses even wield great [[Divine Artifacts|Divine Artifact]]s from this age to this day
