**Illumination** is the rush of divinity the [[Gods]] pour into the world at [[Stonewake Festival|Stonewake]]. Blessings and power meant to last until the next decade descent.

**Blackstone** is the basalt-like stone of [[Mount Steradian]]. It holds Illumination well and is the common magic resource: walls, roads, harvests, hearths, cantrips. Mined after Stonewake, spent over the decade, hauled back to be renewed. Nations rise and fall on its supply.
