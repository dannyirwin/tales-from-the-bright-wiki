Any artifact believed to originate from the gods in the [[Heroic Age]]. They are very rare and can vary significantly in their power or utility. Many are simple magical items that could otherwise be easily created using modern divine magic. Others wll have the power to protect a whole town from the wilds or raise a lucky explorer into the ranks of great heroes and noble families. Many of them do not rely on the [[Divine Illumination|Illumination]] required for other magics of the [[Bright Era - 0 to 2489 (Present)|Modern Era]].
