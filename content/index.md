The campaign wiki for Tales From the Bright
## About this Project

The number one goal of this wiki project is just for me to learn [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/), a program for note taking and organization. Its boring and will probably end up completely ignored by me 3 sessions in. But this wiki is automatically generated and kept up to date with a little setup in GitHub for free and with 0 additional work from me.

That said: **Nothing on the wiki is required reading** after Getting Started. If you never open up this wiki again, beautiful, you will not be expected to know or remember anything. But if it is your thing, anything that gets published to this wiki is considered public knowledge.

This is a living repo, as we write the world I'll add to it


## Getting Started

- Prefer 5.5e but 5e is cool too
- Any classes are fine and most* official heritage/race without a fly speed is good.
- Point buy stats




See the [[World Overview]]

Backstory considerations, no need to answer all/any of these but just to get ideas going:
- You will begin as a part of a caravan on its journey to [[Lumenhold]] for the great [[Stonewake Festival]], what brings you on this pilgrimage?
- Have you been to Lumenhold or to the Festival before?
- Where are you from, and if not from Lumenhold, and whats the deal there?
- Who do you know in the caravan, if anybody?
- How well acquainted to the wilds are you?
- What is your relationship with the gods? Do you follow any?
- What responsibilities are you either fulfilling on this trip, or potentially leaving behind?
