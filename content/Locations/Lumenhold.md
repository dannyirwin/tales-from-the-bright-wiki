City in the crater of [[Mount Steradian]], in [[Arecene]]. It is the pinnacle of the Bright Age, largest city which not only supports a huge population, but also supplies the world with [[Divine Illumination|Illuminated Blackstone]], the infra and manpower to support [[NAME OF GOD ROAD NETWORK]], and generally takes good care of its folk and visitors

Hosts the [[Stonewake Festival]], when on the longest night the Bright make the mountain briefly the brightest place in the world. 

Seat of power for the [[Order of the Bright]]

### The founding
- The first 100 years after the [[Divine Calamity - The War of the Gods|War of the Gods]] were chaos, let alone the first decade after.  Many nations not survive the war, and even more falling in the tumult after, having been abandoned by all gods.  What few sprawling nations remained fell apart and broke off into floundering cities and nomadic groups.
