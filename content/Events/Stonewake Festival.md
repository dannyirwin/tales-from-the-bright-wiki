Stonewake is the decade festival when the [[Gods]] descend to [[Lumenhold]] on [[Mount Steradian]]

The actual Stonewake event falls at midnight on the longest week-long night of the year. Stonewake is the moment [[Arecenia - The Bright Mother|Bright Mother]] [[Divine Illumination|Illuminates]] the [[Divine Illumination|Blackstone]] and children gods pour their divinity into the rest of the world, re-illuminating blessings and artifacts the world over. They say being closer to the gods brings even more potent blessings, but they say a lot of things.

 The festival itself lats for weeks, and the preparations for the festival take years. The population will (at least) tripple, fortunes will rise and fall, and everybody will witness gods.

It is considered a badge of honor, devotion, and bravery to at some point in ones life make the pilgrimage.
