The [[Arecenia - The Bright Mother|Bright Mother]] and Co vs [[XXX - Void Mother|Void Mother]] and Co

Fought over the role of the divine in the lives on the [[Mundane Plane|Mundane World]]. The Void fought to keep freely exerting their influence as they saw fit, keeping deeply intertwined and a destabilizing force in the world. Vs the Bright saw the damange and fought to protect, in the name of stability. The war was drawn out, sometimes subtle and often cataclysmic, and left the wild scarred and suffuce with magic.  [[Arecenia - The Bright Mother|Bright Mother]] defeats the Void and ends the war

The war marks the end of the [[Heroic Age]] and year 0 of the [[Bright Era - 0 to 2489 (Present)]].
