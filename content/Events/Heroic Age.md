An age of magic and adventure. The world was wild and dangerous, but people could still carve a place in it. Gods visited the [[Mundane Plane]] whenever they liked, meddled for good or ill, and could tip a nation into prosperity or ruin on a whim. Magic was everywhere, for folk and monsters alike.

Gods often chose [[Champions]] and gave them [[Divine Artifacts]] and other mythical gifts. Most of those are lost. Some artifacts are still around, held by heroes or their bloodlines. A few are strong enough to shelter whole communities from the wilds.

Ended by the [[Divine Calamity - The War of the Gods]].
