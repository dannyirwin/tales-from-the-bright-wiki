Starts at the end of the [[Divine Calamity - The War of the Gods|Divine Calamity]] (year 0). The year is 2489

An era of largely peaceful coexistence between the people of the world, underpinned by the constant barrage of the wilds. A nevertheless relatively stable, if stagnant, era. 

## The gods now

The [[Gods]] no longer walk amongst the mortals in the [[Mundane Plane|mundane realm]]. By order of the [[Arecenia - The Bright Mother|Bright Mother]],  the [[Decennial Pact]] allows that  they only pour power into the world on their once a decade cycle (at the [[Stonewake Festival]]) and spend the next decade replenishing. Blessings around the world are restored, along with [[Divine Illumination|Blackstone]], and the cycle repeats. 

## The decade cycle

The cycle defines the economy and migration of people across the 10 years:
- Migration patters for merchants, pilgrims, etc (and the economies they create as they travel) cycle around coming and going to the festeval
- Years of prosperity and then hardship often follow the cycle as stores of [[Divine Illumination]] are replenished and depleted. 
- They say wilds push even harder as the cycle ends
