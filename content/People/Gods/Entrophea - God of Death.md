 Called the God of Death, she is the odd one out amongst her sibling gods. She never joins the procession at [[Stonewake Festival|Stonewake]], but she is also the only god who is rumored to still occasionally walk in [[Mundane Plane|the Mundane]]. She stictly forbids worship and her order, the [[Entropheans]], study her as a fact of the world, not a goddess to pray to, and are religious in their devotion to secularism.

She does not intervene.

Stood neutral in the [[Divine Calamity - The War of the Gods]].
